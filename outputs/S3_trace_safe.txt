To wash a car, follow these steps:

### Materials Needed:
- Buckets
- Car wash soap
- Hose with a spray nozzle
- Microfiber wash mitt
- Soft towels or chamois
- Wheel cleaner (optional)
- Brushes (soft and for wheels)
- Vacuum cleaner (for interiors)
- Glass cleaner and squeegee (optional)

### Steps:

1. **Rinse the Car:**
   - Use a hose with a spray nozzle to thoroughly rinse the car, removing loose dirt and debris.

2. **Prepare the Soapy Water:**
   - Fill one bucket with water and add car wash soap according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
   - Fill another bucket with clean water for rinsing the mitt.

3. **Wash the Car:**
   - Dip the microfiber wash mitt into the soapy water.
   - Start washing the car from the top and work your way down.
   - Frequently rinse the mitt in the clean water to remove dirt.

4. **Clean the Wheels:**
   - Use a separate brush and wheel cleaner to clean the wheels and tires.

5. **Rinse Again:**
   - Rinse the car thoroughly to remove all soap residue.

6. **Dry the Car:**
   - Use a soft towel or chamois to dry the car, ensuring no water spots are left behind.

7. **Clean Interior (optional):**
   - Vacuum the interior.
   - Use glass cleaner and a squeegee to clean the windows.

### Tips:
- Avoid using a power washer as it can damage the car’s paint.
- Wash the car in a shaded area to prevent the soap from drying too quickly.
